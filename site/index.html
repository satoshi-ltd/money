01 / One person. One phone.

A month that explains itself.
Nothing to configure.

Môney doesn’t ask for a budget or a goal. It takes what you spent by today, sets it beside what you usually spend by the same day, and says so in a line.

≈

Read against your usual.

The yardstick is you: the median of your previous months, up to six, to the same day. Budgets exist, per category, and never block an entry.

¤

Any currency. One base.

Thirty currencies, crypto and two metals priced per troy ounce. Everything converts to the one you think in at the day’s public rate, and a closed month at its closing day.

⇄

Money moved isn’t money earned.

A swap between your own accounts, or an entry you hide from Analytics, stays in every balance and out of every month figure. Your savings don’t look like spending.

02 / Four views, one ledger

Write it down.
Read it back.

Overview, Accounts, Analytics and Settings. Add an entry in a few taps, then look back a month at a time, in your base currency.

  1. 01

    Add your accounts.

    Pick a base currency, then an account in any of thirty. Its opening balance stays on the account; a card or a loan can sit below zero and is shown as owed.

  2. 02

    Write a title.

    Môney remembers the category and account you gave a title before, and offers its latest amount with a tap. It learns from your own entries and nowhere else.

  3. 03

    Read the month.

    The Overview shows what you’ve spent so far against your usual, the category that moved most, and what you have scheduled. Tap the net worth to mask every amount.

  4. 04

    Look back.

    Analytics draws your balance with its trend, cash flow with incomes above and expenses below, and where it went by category and merchant.

  5. 05

    Keep a copy.

    Export the whole ledger as a JSON file, or the transactions as CSV. Import checks a file before it touches anything, and a bad one never overwrites your ledger.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no subscription and no payment, in the app or anywhere else. Môney makes nothing from your data, because it never receives any.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-in, no sync service and no cloud. Môney opens on your phone with a four-digit PIN, optionally behind your fingerprint or face.

Where is my data?

On the phone, in the app’s own storage. Backups are plain JSON files you own and keep wherever you like; the PIN and the biometric preference never travel in one.

What does Môney send over the network?

One kind of request: the public exchange-rate feed, refreshed when it’s more than six hours old. It carries no ledger data and no identifier, only a request for a public file. Adding, reading and exporting all work offline, and a bundled rate table covers the first launch.

Which currencies can I use?

Thirty: major and regional currencies, five cryptocurrencies, and gold and silver priced per troy ounce. Pick one as your base and change it whenever you like.

Who is it not for?

If you want bank sync, shared budgets, receipts, savings goals or split transactions, Môney is not it, on purpose. You write entries yourself, and it stays one person’s ledger.

How does it judge my month?

It compares what you’ve spent by today with the median of what you had spent by the same day in your previous months, up to six. With enough of the month gone it gives a percentage; earlier it gives a word, more, less or as usual; and with no earlier month to compare, or only one early in the month, it shows the figure alone.

What if I forget my PIN?

Neither the PIN nor the fingerprint or face preference can be recovered, and neither is in a backup. Keep the PIN somewhere safe.

Which languages and sizes?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German, chosen from your device or in Settings, in light or dark, with four text sizes.
